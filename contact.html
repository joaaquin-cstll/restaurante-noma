Restaurant policies

Reservations and cancellations

Free cancellation up to 48 hours in advance. After that, a €30 fee per guest applies to tasting menus. We hold your table for 15 minutes.

Allergies and intolerances

Tell us when booking. We adapt menus with 48 hours' notice; we cannot guarantee the absence of allergen traces.

Children and pets

Children are welcome; high chairs are available. Small dogs are welcome on the terrace.

Groups and events

For groups of more than 7, please contact us directly to arrange a set menu.

Privacy

We use your data only to manage your booking or enquiry, in accordance with the GDPR. To exercise your rights, email hola@noma-restaurante.es.
